Report AI-generated content

Required Microsoft Store disclosure · support@deskii.app

Deskii uses generative AI through the agent service you connect. If you encounter AI-generated output that is harmful, misleading, illegal, or violates our Terms of Use, please report it.

What to report

  • Content that is harmful, abusive, threatening, or discriminatory
  • Misleading or false information presented as fact
  • Content facilitating illegal activity
  • Content violating another person's rights
  • Unexpected or unsafe tool-call behaviour by the connected agent

How to report

Email support@deskii.app with subject: AI Content Report

Please include: a description of the content and why it is problematic; the approximate date and time; your Deskii version (shown in the Control Center footer); the agent type you were using; and a screenshot or copy of the output if safe to share.

We review all reports and respond within 5 business days. Where content originates from a connected third-party agent, we may forward the report to that agent's provider.

Important notes

  • Deskii does not operate the underlying AI model — it is an interface to the agent service you connect.
  • XEON Protocol cannot modify outputs of third-party agent services, but we investigate whether Deskii's interface contributed and escalate to the agent provider.
  • For safety emergencies, contact local emergency services. This form is not monitored in real time.
