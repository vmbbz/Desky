Privacy Policy

XEON Protocol (Pty) Ltd · Last updated: 2 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: Deskii stores your agent credentials locally on your device. Your conversation content goes directly between you and the agent service you choose — Deskii does not read, store, or transmit it. We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the app and optional hosted services.

1. Who we are

XEON Protocol (Pty) Ltd is a company registered in South Africa under the Companies Act 71 of 2008. We are the responsible party for the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Contact: privacy@deskii.app

2. What Deskii does and does not do

Deskii is a desktop interface that connects to AI agent services you configure. The app:

  • Does not read, log, or forward your conversation prompts or agent replies.
  • Does not receive your wallet seed phrase, private key, or signing authority.
  • Does not collect usage telemetry or analytics in the base app.
  • Does store your agent connection credentials locally in the app's main process, isolated from the renderer.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Stored on your device only

DataPurposeLocation

None of this is sent to XEON Protocol servers.

3.2 Account and entitlement data (optional hosted features)

If you create a Deskii account for the avatar marketplace, we store via Supabase: email address and hashed password (or OAuth token reference), purchased entitlements, account creation date and last sign-in timestamp.

3.3 Commerce data (x402 direct payments — future feature)

When direct-download builds enable x402 payments: your public wallet address and transaction hashes are recorded as purchase evidence. Deskii never receives your private key or seed phrase — all signing is done in MetaMask on your device.

3.4 Support communications

When you contact support@deskii.app we receive and retain your email and message content, used solely to respond to your request.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not use advertising networks.

5. Data retention

  • Local app data: retained until you uninstall Deskii
  • Account data: deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Support emails: retained up to 2 years, then deleted
  • On-chain records: permanently public on the blockchain; our copy deleted within 90 days of account deletion

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator. To exercise these rights: account deletion or privacy@deskii.app.

7. Children

Deskii is not directed at children under 18. Contact privacy@deskii.app if you believe a child has provided us personal information.
