# Desky first-signal poster prompt

## Reference role

The attached image is the identity reference for Milk, Desky's founding mascot. Preserve the recognizable low-poly milk-carton body, folded carton top, white upper carton, pale cyan middle band, medium blue lower panel, simple black dot eyes and curved smile, black `MILK` label, and very thin off-white arms and legs. Do not redesign the character, add clothing, or make it photorealistic.

## Generation prompt

Create premium cinematic launch key art for a desktop companion named Desky, in a wide 16:9 social-media poster composition. Show Milk as a small, friendly, full-body low-poly figure standing just right of center on the barely visible edge of a dark glass desktop surface. The environment is deep midnight navy and near-black, quiet and spacious, with a single soft mint-teal signal blooming behind and beneath the character. Add an extremely subtle constellation of fine geometric nodes and one faint circular authorization ripple to suggest an onchain access key, never cryptocurrency speculation. Milk should feel newly awake, curious, and gently alive, with a slight head tilt and relaxed asymmetrical pose. Use restrained teal and soft blue highlights that connect to Milk's colors. Leave generous clean negative space in the upper-left and left-center for a logo and short headline.

Visual direction: world-class minimal technology brand campaign, editorial lighting, refined 3D render, premium product reveal, subtle film grain, crisp silhouette, believable soft contact shadow, strong hierarchy, calm rather than cyberpunk. The background should be abstract and desktop-like, not an office and not outer space.

Avoid: any rendered text except the existing `MILK` label on the character, speech bubbles, app windows, computer hardware, coins, currency symbols, Ethereum or Base logos, chains, locks, code, contract documents, NFT imagery, neon overload, hologram clutter, extra characters, human hands, exaggerated cartoon expression, glossy toy plastic, or a busy background.
