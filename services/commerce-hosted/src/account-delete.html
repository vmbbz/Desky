Request account deletion

Your rights under POPIA · privacy@desky.app

You may request deletion of your Deskii account and associated personal data at any time. We process requests within 30 days and confirm by email when complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account email address and credentials
  • Purchased entitlements and licence records
  • Support correspondence associated with your account (after statutory retention period)
  • XEON Protocol's copy of purchase evidence (within 90 days)

What cannot be deleted

  • On-chain transaction records — blockchain transactions are permanent by nature. If you made an x402 payment, the on-chain record (wallet address, amount, timestamp) exists independently of Deskii.
  • Legal retention obligations — certain records may be retained as required by South African law.

Local app data

Account deletion removes server-side data. To remove local data (credentials, preferences, avatar cache), uninstall Deskii and delete: %APPDATA%\Deskii

How to request

Email privacy@desky.app with subject: Account Deletion Request
Include the email address on your Deskii account. We may verify your identity before processing.

We acknowledge within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days. For other data rights requests, use the same address. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
